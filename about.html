Who we are

An NGO and think tank for safer, stronger families.

Founded in 2012, the AustralAsian Centre for Human Rights & Health proposes innovative, evidence-led solutions to prevent family violence — with a focus on dowry abuse and coercive control in migrant communities.

Mission & story

Empowering communities through education and research.

ACHRH is an NGO and think tank that proposes innovative solutions to build safer and stronger families. Since 2012 we have combined clinical insight, research and participatory art to prevent harm — and to change the systems and laws that allow it.

“There is no higher form of contact with humans than art.”
