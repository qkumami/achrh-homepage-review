What changed because ACHRH acted

Evidence into recognition, recognition into law.

Since 2012, ACHRH has turned community experience into research, advocacy and submissions — and helped make dowry abuse a recognised form of family violence in Victorian law.

How we changed law & policy

From a community campaign to a change in the law.

ACHRH advocated, submitted evidence and petitioned; the Royal Commission recommended reform; the Victorian Parliament legislated it.

Community experience→Research & evidence→ Advocacy & petition→Royal Commission submission→ Recommendation 156→Victorian law reform
2013
ACHRH initiates its anti-dowry campaign, grounded in action research & clinical evidence
2014
Petition tabled in Victorian Parliament by former Premier Ted Baillieu
2015
ACHRH submits to the Royal Commission into Family Violence (10 May)
2016
Royal Commission report acknowledges ACHRH's submission; Recommendation 156
2018
Amendment passes both houses of Victorian Parliament (7 Dec)
2019
Law proclaimed — dowry-related abuse a statutory example, Family Violence Protection Act 2008 (29 Mar)
