SNEH Theatre · 2021–2024

Eight performances. One living conversation.

Community volunteer actors perform scenarios about seeking help early — and the audience helps write the ending.

SNEH Theatre Verified public source

Culturally responsive, community-led.

Funded by a Victorian Government grant (2021–2024), SNEH Theatre delivered eight theatre performances for the South Asian community across Greater Melbourne (2022–2024), alongside theatre workshops that trained community members to become actors.

Audiences “actively participate by commenting via a mediator, critically analysing the presentation” — exploring what help-seeking involves, where to go, and how to navigate the system, while recognising the laws around family violence and dowry abuse.
