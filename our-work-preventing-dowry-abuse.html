Preventing Dowry Abuse

A decade of community education, research and advocacy that helped make dowry abuse a recognised form of family violence in Victorian law.

Why this matters

Dowry abuse is family violence.

Dowry-related coercion, financial abuse and violence have cost lives in Australia's migrant communities. ACHRH set out to name the harm, support victim-survivors, and change the systems that allowed it to go unrecognised.

“Do not blame the victims of domestic violence — support them.”

Editorial image — asset to supply
What we did — and what changed

Activity, then outcome.

What ACHRH did

Launched a national anti-dowry campaign, ran community education and summits, and made evidence-based submissions to the Royal Commission and a Senate inquiry.

What changed

Dowry abuse was recognised in Victorian family-violence law (2019), and dowry/coercive-control evidence informed national policy.

The law-reform journey

Milestone by milestone.
