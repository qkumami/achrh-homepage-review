Supporting someone else

Help someone I know

If you're worried about someone, your support can make a real difference.

Listen without judgement

Believe them, let them talk at their pace, and don't pressure them to act.

Respect their choices

Leaving can be dangerous and complex. Support their decisions and safety.

Share support options

Let them know about 1800RESPECT and ACHRH's resources — when they're ready.

If someone is in immediate danger

  • Call 000 if there is an immediate threat to safety.
  • 1800RESPECT (1800 737 732) can advise you on how to help someone else, too.
