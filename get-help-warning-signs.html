Recognising harm

Understand the warning signs

Family violence isn't only physical. Here are signs to be aware of.

Emotional abuse

Being put down, controlled, isolated from friends and family, or made to feel afraid.

Financial & dowry abuse

Coercive dowry demands, control of money, no access to a joint account, or being made financially dependent.

Threats & coercion

Threats to your safety, to cancel a visa, or to withhold or manipulate children.

In ACHRH's words

ACHRH's public materials describe dowry-related coercion and threats — including visa-cancellation threats and using children as threats — as forms of family and economic abuse that can cause serious stress and trauma. Content to verify against final ACHRH copy
