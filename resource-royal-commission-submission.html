Government submission · Victorian Royal Commission into Family Violence

Refusal to comply with dowry demands contributes to family violence and death in Victoria

ACHRH's 2015 submission set out the clinical and community evidence that dowry-related coercion is family violence — evidence the Commission acknowledged, and that informed Recommendation 156.

Why this matters

Naming a hidden harm.

Drawing on action research and clinical practice, ACHRH argued that coercive dowry demands are a form of economic and family violence — with severe, sometimes fatal, consequences in Victoria's migrant communities.

The Commission's 2016 report noted that “dowry plays a significant adverse role in leading to emotional and physical abuse.”

What changed

The submission was acknowledged in the Commission's report; Recommendation 156 proposed amending the Family Violence Protection Act 2008. In 2019, dowry-related abuse became a statutory example of family violence in Victorian law.
