Signs of a healthy relationship From ACHRH's material

Nine things that matter.

Respect

Listening without judgement and valuing each other's opinions.

Equal responsibility

A fair, mutually agreed share of housework.

Negotiation & fairness

Seeking resolutions to conflict that satisfy you both.

Empathy & compassion

Recognising how threatening behaviour induces fear.

Honesty & accountability

Accepting responsibility for one's own behaviour.

Social freedom

Freedom to make friends and keep family contact without restriction.

Economic partnership

Making money decisions together, with joint account access.

Responsible parenting

Sharing parenting without manipulating children.

No threats

Talking and acting so that you feel safe and comfortable.

If some of these are missing

  • You deserve to feel safe, respected and free.
  • ACHRH's materials suggest speaking to your GP if you'd like counselling.
  • You can call 1800RESPECT (1800 737 732) at any time — free and confidential.

This page is informational and self-reflective only. It is not a diagnosis, risk score, or assessment, and nothing here is submitted or stored.
