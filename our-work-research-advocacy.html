Research & Advocacy

Peer-reviewed research and parliamentary submissions that turned community experience into evidence — and evidence into policy.

Why this matters

Evidence is how a small centre moves a nation.

ACHRH publishes in medical and psychiatric journals and makes submissions to Royal Commissions and Senate inquiries. Each artefact is authored once and referenced across our work — from theatre to law reform.

Research → outcome

From evidence to law.

What ACHRH did

Published the health and public-health case for treating dowry abuse as family violence, and submitted evidence to the Royal Commission and Senate.

What changed

The evidence underpinned Recommendation 156 and the 2019 recognition of dowry abuse in Victorian family-violence law.
