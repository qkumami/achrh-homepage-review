You are not alone

Get help.

If you or someone you know may be experiencing family violence, dowry abuse or coercive control, support is available — right now.

Call 1800RESPECT — 1800 737 732

The national domestic, family and sexual violence counselling service — 24 hours, 7 days. You can also text 0458 737 732 or chat online.

In immediate danger? Call 000

Police, fire and ambulance emergency.

More support services
